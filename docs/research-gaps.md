# Research Gap Analysis

The registry can eventually generate evidence-backed maps of missing or underrepresented resources.

Potential dimensions include:

- language and dialect
- geography
- age group
- therapy modality
- clinical topic
- longitudinal structure
- annotation type
- access level
- privacy evaluation
- audio/video availability

A gap should be reported as an observed absence or sparse representation in the registry, not as proof that no dataset exists anywhere.
