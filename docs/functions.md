# Registry Functions

The Global Psychotherapy Transcript Data Registry is designed as multi-function research infrastructure rather than a simple dataset list.

## 1. Discovery
Find psychotherapy and therapy-adjacent dialogue datasets by language, geography, modality, population, clinical topic, longitudinal structure, access, privacy characteristics, and historical period.

## 2. Historical mapping
Map psychotherapy data across time to study how therapeutic modalities, clinical language, therapist practices, and client expression changed across eras.

## 3. Modality research
Support comparison of observed therapeutic interaction across psychodynamic, CBT, humanistic/person-centered, motivational interviewing, relational, systemic, and integrative approaches.

## 4. Longitudinal research
Identify datasets that preserve repeated sessions, participant/session linkage, treatment duration, or temporal ordering.

## 5. Clinical-process research
Index datasets containing annotations or metadata for therapist interventions, client change talk, emotion, alliance, risk, symptoms, therapeutic process, or other clinically relevant constructs.

## 6. Cultural and linguistic research
Track source language, dialect, code-switching, cultural context, geography, migration/diaspora context, and whether language is original, translated, parallel, or multilingual.

## 7. Privacy research
Record de-identification methods, access restrictions, contextual re-identification assessment, privacy attacks, and downstream utility evaluation where documented.

## 8. AI evaluation
Help researchers locate datasets appropriate for evaluating transcription, therapeutic-process modeling, multilingual interpretation, privacy-preserving transformation, longitudinal analysis, and other computational tasks.

## 9. Research-gap mapping
Aggregate registry metadata to identify underrepresented combinations of language, geography, modality, population, era, clinical topic, longitudinal structure, access, and privacy evaluation.

## 10. Therapy-knowledge evolution
Support study of how therapeutic concepts and conversational practices change over time.

## 11. Evidence and provenance
Every substantive registry claim should be traceable to explicit evidence. Unknown is a valid value.

## 12. Responsible access
The registry indexes datasets. It does not redistribute copyrighted, restricted, confidential, or participant-protected transcripts.

## Design principle

One canonical dataset record can power many derived views:

`DATASET → evidence-backed metadata → search → timeline → coverage analysis → research discovery → privacy/ethics analysis`
