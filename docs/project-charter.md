# Project Charter

## Mission
Create a transparent, machine-readable map of psychotherapy transcript and dialogue datasets without redistributing data that the registry is not authorized to redistribute.

## Research questions
- What psychotherapy dialogue datasets exist?
- Which are real, synthetic, or hybrid?
- Which languages, populations, modalities, and clinical topics are represented?
- Which datasets are longitudinal?
- What annotations and downstream tasks are supported?
- What access and licensing constraints exist?
- What privacy/de-identification evidence is documented?
- Where are the research gaps?

## Non-goals
The registry does not certify anonymity, privacy, legal compliance, ethical approval, or fitness for a particular study.
