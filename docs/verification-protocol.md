# Verification Protocol

A record is verified when key claims can be traced to authoritative sources.

1. Confirm dataset identity and canonical source.
2. Confirm real/synthetic/hybrid status.
3. Confirm transcript/audio/video availability.
4. Confirm published counts.
5. Confirm language, modality, population, and longitudinal properties where documented.
6. Confirm access pathway.
7. Confirm license and redistribution conditions.
8. Record privacy/de-identification statements as supported.
9. Record publications and reuse.
10. Record the verification date.

Unknown values remain unknown. Never fill gaps by inference.
