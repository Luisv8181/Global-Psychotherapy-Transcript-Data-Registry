# Research Functions

The registry can serve multiple research workflows without distributing restricted source data.

## Dataset discovery

Natural-language discovery examples:

- longitudinal psychotherapy datasets with 100+ sessions
- real therapy transcripts in Spanish
- multilingual therapy dialogue with cultural annotations
- historical psychotherapy recordings with transcripts
- datasets containing therapist intervention labels
- datasets suitable for privacy-preserving transformation evaluation

## Cross-dataset comparison

Compare metadata, not raw restricted transcripts, across modality, era, language, geography, population, clinical topic, longitudinal structure, annotation, access, and privacy method.

## Evidence graph

`DATASET ↔ PUBLICATION ↔ REPOSITORY ↔ PROVIDER ↔ MODALITY ↔ POPULATION ↔ LANGUAGE ↔ ACCESS ↔ PRIVACY METHOD`

## Research-question routing

A future API should convert a research question into metadata filters and return matching datasets, evidence, access conditions, known limitations, publications, and privacy/ethics considerations.

## Meta-analysis readiness

The registry does not perform statistical meta-analysis automatically. It provides structured provenance and metadata needed to determine whether cross-dataset synthesis is scientifically defensible.

## Privacy-aware research discovery

Search results should distinguish open raw transcript, open metadata only, institutional access, controlled research access, restricted/private, synthetic, hybrid, and unknown.

## AI benchmark discovery

Future task-oriented subsets can cover therapeutic-process classification, intervention recognition, emotion analysis, alliance research, risk-language research, multilingual counseling NLP, speech/transcription evaluation, semantic de-identification, contextual re-identification testing, and longitudinal change analysis.
