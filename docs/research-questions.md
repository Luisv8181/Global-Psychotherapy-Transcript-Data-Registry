# Research Questions the Registry Can Support

## Discovery

What psychotherapy dialogue datasets exist, and where are they hosted?

## Representation

Which languages, dialects, populations, regions, therapy modalities, age groups, and clinical topics are represented?

## Longitudinal structure

Which datasets contain repeated sessions or repeated observations from the same participants?

## Annotation

Which corpora contain therapist strategies, client change talk, emotion, alliance, risk, intervention, outcome, or turn-level annotations?

## Access

How much psychotherapy dialogue is open, registration-based, institutionally licensed, or controlled access?

## Privacy

Which datasets document de-identification, pseudonymization, privacy evaluation, or re-identification testing?

## Research gaps

Where are the underrepresented languages, populations, therapy modalities, longitudinal designs, and annotation types?

## Meta-research

How do dataset access, privacy constraints, and downstream research utility interact?

The registry records the evidence needed to investigate these questions. It does not answer them automatically without analysis of the underlying records.
