How to read a record

Discovery is not verification.

Primary evidenceFacts come from the dataset's own pages, files or paper. Summaries are leads, not evidence.
Unknown is a valid answerMissing evidence is recorded as unknown, never guessed. An unknown field means “not yet documented here”.
The dataset's license, not the paper'sA journal article's open license says nothing about the data. Each license cites where it was read.
Provenance stays distinctReal sessions, teaching demonstrations, peer support, synthetic and derived data are never merged.
Discoverable ≠ redistributableAccess and redistribution are tracked separately. Check the provider's current terms before use.
Not an ethics approvalThe registry does not certify anonymity, consent or legal use. Those remain the researcher's responsibility.
