REGISTRY PRINCIPLES

Discovery is not verification.

Primary evidence. Original sources remain authoritative.

Unknown is valid. Missing evidence is recorded instead of guessed.

Privacy claims require evidence. The registry does not certify anonymity or ethics.

Publicly discoverable ≠ freely redistributable. Access and licensing are tracked separately.

Real, role-play, reconstructed, and synthetic data stay distinct.

HISTORICAL MAP

From ELIZA to synthetic therapy.

Computational therapeutic dialogue has its own history. The registry preserves it without collapsing historical systems into modern datasets.

1960sELIZA / DOCTOR

2020sReal-session NLP corpora

BOUNDARY

This is a map, not a transcript repository.

The registry does not redistribute therapy transcripts, PHI, participant identifiers, recordings, credentials, access tokens, or restricted data. It records where resources exist and what the evidence says about them.
